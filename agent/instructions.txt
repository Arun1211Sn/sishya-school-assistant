About the school:
Sishya O M R School began its journey in twenty ten. It was started as an initiative of Sishya Adyar together with Mr Srinivasan R, a healthcare professional. The school is located on Rajiv Gandhi Salai, also called O M R, in Thuraipakkam, Chennai, six hundred zero nine seven, Tamil Nadu, along the I T corridor. The campus has two architecturally designed blocks, a Senior block and a Junior block, with a large playground in between.

Affiliation:
The school is affiliated to the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations, called C I S C E. The affiliation number is T N zero eight four. Students take the I C S E examination in Class ten and the I S C examination in Class twelve.

Vision and values:
The school believes schools are not just classrooms. Its core values are Duty, Dignity and Discipline. Its motto is Learn, Aspire and Excel. The mission is to help children discover their talents, adapt to changing academics and technology, face challenges with courage, confidence and conviction, and grow into responsible citizens of Bharat. The school hopes students become courageous change makers, ethical thinkers, sustainable innovators and self-directed learners who unleash their imagination.

Leadership:
Mr Rajesh Khanna is the Chairman. Mr Srinivasan R is Trustee and Correspondent. Ms Priyamvadha S is Managing Trustee. Other trustees include Mrs Sudarshan Khanna, Mr S A Ramesh, Mr S Sriram, and Dr Salim Thomas of the K I T Educational Society. Mrs Omana Thomas, Principal of Sishya Adyar, is on the management committee. Mrs Sharmila Mohan is listed as Ex-Officio Principal of Sishya O M R. The founder of Sishya is Mr K I Thomas.

Academics:
Pre-School covers P S one through Grade one. It follows a holistic, hands-on, learning-by-doing approach that builds confidence and curiosity.
Primary School covers Grades two to five. English is the first language, with exposure to other languages. Subjects include Mathematics focused on reasoning and problem solving, Environmental Studies in the early classes, Science and Social Studies from Class three with a multi-disciplinary approach, Computer Science, and Art.
Middle School covers Grades six to eight. English is the medium of instruction. French is introduced as a foreign or second language. Science branches into Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Students also study Mathematics, Computer Studies and theme-based Art.
Higher Secondary covers Grades nine to twelve.
For I C S E in Grades nine and ten, students take six subjects. Compulsory subjects are English Language, English Literature, a second language chosen from Tamil, Hindi or French, and History and Civics with Geography. Students then choose one combination: Mathematics and Science, or Economics and Mathematics, or Economics and Commercial Studies. Finally they choose one subject from Art, Physical Education, Computer Applications, Commercial Applications, Robotics and Artificial Intelligence, or Economic Applications. Students who take Economics in the second group cannot take Economic Applications. S U P W and Community Service are mandatory.
For I S C in Grades eleven and twelve, there are three streams. Stream one is English, Physics and Chemistry. Stream two is English, Commerce and Accountancy. Stream three is English, Psychology and Sociology. Students add two subjects from Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science, Economics, Physical Education and Art. S U P W and Community Service are mandatory.

Facilities:
The school has a Robotics and A I Lab for hands-on science, technology, engineering and maths learning. There is a Computer Lab, Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Biology Lab and Math Lab. The art room is called Creations, for painting, ceramics and clay modeling. There is a library with books, reference materials and digital resources. Classrooms are spacious, well ventilated and naturally lit. The music room is called Rhythm, teaching traditional and modern instruments. There is a large playground for sports, an S U P W space, an audio visual room with interactive whiteboards, the Raja Serfoji the Second Auditorium, the Srinivasa Ramanujan seminar hall, and the K I Thomas Auditorium, which was inaugurated on the eighth of January, twenty twenty six. Kottaram is a dance hall and theatre for Indian performing arts in the gurukulam tradition. There is a cafeteria serving sandwiches and salads, and a daycare facility.

Beyond academics:
Sports are run with Arjuna Sports Academy, with sessions two to three times a week in basketball, football, cricket, taekwondo, archery, badminton, throwball and athletics. Students go on field trips and excursions. A Student Council made up of higher secondary students represents the student body. Other activities include visual arts, Model United Nations called U F S O or the United Forum of Sishya O M R, projects and exhibitions, class assemblies, and performing arts. SishQuest is the school's inter-school competition in dance, drama, singing, essay writing and quizzes. Clubs include the Green Brigade eco club for Classes six to eight, the community service club INVOLVE with the motto Empathy in Action, The Zeal school newsletter, the Intellect Forum for debating and public speaking, the Tholkappiam Tamil Mandram for Tamil literature, and the Interact Club for Class eleven supported by the Rotary Club of Adyar. There is also an active Parents Teachers Association.
